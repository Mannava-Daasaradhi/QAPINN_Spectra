\section{Benchmark: Poisson}
The Poisson benchmark, with solution $\sin\pi x + 0.3\sin15\pi x$, mixes a slow and a
fast wave, the case spectral bias makes hard. On the held-out seeds the circuit reaches a
median error of 0.37\%, with every seed below 2\%, and these locked predictions hold on
all five seeds:
\begin{itemize}
  \item beats the same circuit with random frequencies by \textbf{236$\times$};
  \item beats a Fourier-feature network by \textbf{three orders of magnitude};
  \item 99.9\% of its improvement lands on the designed frequencies;
  \item its NTK is flatter inside $\Omega$ than outside, as the theory predicts;
  \item its encoder stays put during training, so the design stays valid.
\end{itemize}
