\subsection*{The locked re-test on fresh seeds}
Eight of v1's claims, word for word, were re-tested on seeds 10--14 with the v2 circuit,
after the predictions were pushed to GitHub. \textbf{\VTwoConf{} of 8 held.}
\begin{center}
  \includegraphics[width=0.92\linewidth]{headline_v2_retest}
\end{center}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Held:} beats Fourier features; beats the same circuit with random
    frequencies (236$\times$); its improvement lands on the designed frequencies; the NTK
    is flatter inside $\Omega$ than outside; the encoder stays put.
  \item \textbf{Did not hold:} a classical model with the \emph{same} frequencies and
    nearly the same size (83 parameters) is still 3$\times$ more accurate; the circuit does
    not leave the lazy-training regime earlier.
  \item \textbf{Inconclusive:} against the MLP. The MLP at its tuned learning rate failed on
    three of five fresh seeds and did well on two, so the seeds do not agree.
  \item \textbf{Caveat:} the classical learning rates chosen in tuning did not carry over
    to new seeds (the MLP and Fourier features were unstable), while every v2 circuit run
    landed below 2\% error.
\end{itemize}
