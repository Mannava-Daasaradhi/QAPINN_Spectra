\section{Benchmark: Poisson}
The Poisson benchmark, with solution $\sin\pi x + 0.3\sin15\pi x$ mixes a slow and a
fast wave, the case spectral bias makes hard. Eight predictions were locked before the
held-out runs; \textbf{\VTwoConf{} of 8 held.}
\begin{center}
  \includegraphics[width=0.92\linewidth]{headline_v2_retest}
\end{center}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Held:} beats the same circuit with random frequencies (236$\times$) and
    Fourier features (1{,}360$\times$); its improvement lands on the designed frequencies
    (99.9\%); the NTK is flatter inside $\Omega$ than outside; the encoder stays put
    during training.
  \item \textbf{Did not hold:} a classical model given the same frequencies (83
    parameters) is 3$\times$ more accurate here; the circuit does not leave the lazy
    training regime earlier.
  \item \textbf{Inconclusive:} against the large MLP, which failed on three of five
    held-out seeds and did well on two.
\end{itemize}
Every one of the circuit's runs landed below 2\% error, while the MLP and Fourier
features were unstable at the learning rates tuning chose for them.
