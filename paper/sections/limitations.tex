\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

Stated plainly, not hedged:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Statistical power, worse than planned.} The core matrix was
  \emph{pre-registered} at $n=5$ seeds per (problem, family) cell, which cannot reach
  $p<0.05$ on its own (\texttt{src/qapinn/stats.py}, T4.1: the $n=5$ Wilcoxon $p$-floor
  is $0.0625$). Under the Aug 7 submission deadline, the seed count was cut to $n=2$
  (\texttt{docs/plan/08\_TASK\_INDEX.md}'s cut-line, invoked twice), which lowers the
  achievable $p$-floor further, to $0.5$ (verified directly against
  \texttt{scipy.stats.wilcoxon}). \texttt{docs/predictions.md}'s CONFIRMED gate was left
  at its original, pre-registered $0.0625$ threshold rather than loosened to compensate,
  which means every ratio-and-significance prediction (PR-1, PR-2, PR-6) can now only
  resolve to REFUTED or INCONCLUSIVE, independent of true effect size --- stated here
  explicitly rather than left implicit in a table of verdicts.
\item \textbf{Simulation only.} All quantum circuits are evaluated on this project's own
  statevector simulator (\texttt{qsim.py}), cross-checked against a PennyLane oracle to
  $<10^{-10}$; no results here were run on physical quantum hardware.
\item \textbf{Qubit budget.} Experiments are capped at $\le 8$ qubits, dictated by
  classical simulation cost, not by the design methodology itself --- SMCD's depth/width
  formulas (Section~\ref{sec:methodology}) apply unchanged at larger $n$.
\item \textbf{Noise models are surrogates.} Shot-noise and depolarizing noise are analytic
  surrogates (T2.11), not a full density-matrix simulation; the discrepancy between the
  surrogate and a density-matrix reference on a representative subset is measured and
  reported (T4.4), not assumed negligible.
\item \textbf{Scope cut.} A fifth PDE (Allen--Cahn / wave, stretch goal P5) was cut from
  the critical path under the project's time budget; nothing in Section~\ref{sec:methodology}
  is specific to the four PDEs actually run.
\item \textbf{The affine-encoder constraint (D3).} The classical encoder $E_\varphi$ is
  restricted to an affine map, imposed to keep SMCD's coverage metric well-defined (a
  nonlinear encoder would make ``the circuit's realized frequency set'' itself
  input-dependent); this is a real restriction on the hybrid model's expressivity, not a
  free simplification.
\item \textbf{\texttt{predicted\_benefit} is a heuristic.} SMCD's a-priori benefit
  estimate (Section~\ref{sec:algorithm}'s design card, plotted in \texttt{decision\_map}) is a
  provisional heuristic, not a proven bound; Section~\ref{sec:results} reports how well it
  tracks the measured benefit, and a heuristic that tracks poorly is itself a reported
  finding, not hidden.
\item \textbf{PR-10 (barren-plateau decay rate) is unmeasurable on this run's data, and
  reported as such rather than computed anyway.} \texttt{depth\_sweep}'s grid was cut
  under the same deadline pressure to $n_{\text{qubits}}=6$ only (dropping the originally
  planned $n_{\text{qubits}}=4$ point); \texttt{barren\_plateau\_fit} needs at least two
  distinct $n_{\text{qubits}}$ values to fit a slope, and now detects this degenerate
  case explicitly (\texttt{src/qapinn/xai/gradvar.py}) rather than returning the
  ill-conditioned solver artifact \texttt{numpy.polyfit} would otherwise silently produce
  against a constant independent variable. The practical frontier (largest $n$ before
  grad-var collapses below the $10^{-10}$ floor) is unaffected and still reported; only
  the decay-rate metric is the casualty.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{An infrastructure limitation worth stating explicitly.} The core-matrix sweep
this project's results are drawn from ran on a single consumer GPU under real memory and
process-stability constraints, and required several crash-recovery restarts over the
course of Phase 3. The external retry mechanism resumes correctly by run-completion
(\texttt{metrics.json} existence), but a known consequence is that a small fraction of
runs' cumulative per-checkpoint diagnostic files (\texttt{specerr.npz}) were appended to
across a crash boundary, producing duplicate checkpoint entries in that specific artifact
(10 of 84 core-matrix runs, detected mechanically by
\texttt{scripts/check\_specerr\_integrity.py}). Rather than exclude these runs, the
duplicate entries were deduplicated post hoc
(\texttt{scripts/dedupe\_specerr.py}, run once all sweeps had finished so the fix could
not race a still-writing task): for each of the affected files, only the last --- most
recent, post-crash-recovery --- row for each real checkpoint step is kept, discarding
the stale row written before the crash. This is a mechanical cleanup of an accumulation
artifact, not new data; it recovered two figures (\texttt{freq\_heatmap\_poisson},
\texttt{freq\_heatmap\_helmholtz\_k4}) and one prediction (PR-8/poisson, from
INSUFFICIENT\_DATA to a real CONFIRMED at 71.3\% overlap) that would otherwise have been
reported as missing or excluded rather than fixed.
